Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

The short version: Operator runs on your computer, and we do not have a server that holds your work. What the app sees stays on your machine unless you send it somewhere yourself.

The app on your computer

  • Your files, your logins and what is on your screen stay on your PC. We never receive them.
  • The record of what the agent did — the audit log — is written to your own disk, and passwords and card numbers are replaced with a fingerprint before anything is stored.
  • If the app crashes, a report is saved on your computer. It is only sent anywhere if you choose to send it, and secrets are stripped out of it first.

The AI model you choose

To do a task, Operator sends what that task needs — your instruction, and the screen or page it is working on — to the model provider whose key you set: Anthropic for Claude, or NVIDIA. That happens under your own account with them and their privacy terms. We are not in the middle of it, and we do not keep a copy.

This website

  • If you join the launch list, we keep the email address you give us, to tell you when Operator ships. Ask us to remove it any time.
  • When you buy, the payment is taken by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Your card details go to Paddle, never to us; we see only that a sale happened, and enough to give support and a refund.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking on this site.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you — a launch-list email, or a purchase record — email support. Payment records held by Paddle are covered by Paddle's own policy, linked above.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to support, and a person will answer.
