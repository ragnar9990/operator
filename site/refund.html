Refund policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Operator is a one-time purchase, and it comes with a full refund for 14 days from the day you buy it — no questions asked. If it is not for you, tell us within two weeks and you get your money back.

How to ask for one

Email support from the address you bought with, or with your order number. That is all we need. We start the refund as soon as we read it, and you do not have to explain why.

How the money comes back

Payments and refunds are handled by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. A refund goes back to the same card or account you paid with, usually within five to ten business days once it is issued — the exact timing is your bank's, not ours.

What a refund does

Refunding your purchase ends your licence to keep using Operator. You are welcome to buy it again later at the price on the day.

What we cannot refund

Operator runs on your own AI provider key — Anthropic for Claude, or NVIDIA. Those companies bill you directly for what the model does, on their own terms, and we have no part in that charge and cannot refund it. You control that spending from your own account with them.

Questions

Anything at all about a payment or a refund: email support and a person will answer.
